# Weekend Trip: Seattle to Yakima, Palouse Falls & Tri-Cities

## Best Time to Visit

- **Ideal: Late August** (coincides with Yakima Festival of Balloons, Labor Day weekend)
- **Good: Late May - early October** (dry, warm, 300 days of sunshine in Tri-Cities)
- **Peak: September - early October** (harvest season in wine country, golden grasslands, comfortable 70s-80s, fewer crowds)
- **Spring (May-June):** Wildflowers in the shrub-steppe, Palouse Falls at peak flow from snowmelt
- **Avoid:** December-February (cold, some facilities closed, roads can be icy on SR-26)

---

## Yakima Festival of Balloons

The **Yakima Festival of Balloons** is one of the largest balloon festivals in the Pacific Northwest, typically held over **Labor Day weekend (late August/early September)** at **McWhorter Park** in Yakima, WA.

### Highlights:
- **Morning Balloon Launches** (~6:00-7:00 AM) - Watch dozens of colorful hot air balloons lift off at dawn
- **Evening Balloon Glow** (~7:00-8:00 PM) - Balloons inflate and glow in sequence, creating a stunning light show (no launch, just the glow)
- **Food & Craft Vendors** - Local food trucks and artisan booths
- **Carnival Rides & Games** - Family-friendly amusement area
- **Live Entertainment** - Music and performances throughout the day
- **Evening Concert** - Often features a headline act after the balloon glow

### Tips:
- Arrive early for morning launches (gates open around 5:00 AM)
- Bring blankets or lawn chairs for the evening glow
- The event is free to enter; rides and food cost extra
- Parking is at McWhorter Park or nearby shuttles
- **Book Yakima hotels early** - this is the busiest weekend of the year
- Check [visityakima.com](https://www.visityakima.com) for exact dates and details each year

## Friday - Drive to Yakima

### Leave Seattle (afternoon)

- **Depart:** ~2:00 PM (or earlier for a full evening)
- **Route:** I-90 East via Snoqualmie Pass

### Stop: Ellensburg (optional, +30 min detour)

- Historic downtown with antique shops and brick storefronts
- Grab coffee or an early dinner
- **Ginkgo Petrified Forest State Park** (Vantage, WA) - 15 min north of I-90 at Exit 136. One of the world's largest petrified wood collections. Short interpretive trails with views of the Columbia River. Free day-use area; $10/day for parking. Worth the detour if time allows.

### Arrive Yakima (~2.5 hrs from Seattle)

- Check in to hotel

### Evening in Yakima

- Stroll **Historic Downtown Yakima** - murals, local shops, restaurants
- Dinner at a downtown spot (The Mocha House, Hops & Crafts, or Iron Horse Brewery)
- Walk the **Yakima Greenway** along the river (12+ miles of paved trails)

### Friday Hotel (Yakima)

- Book a hotel with hot tub in Yakima (2+ stars)
- Options to search: Hampton Inn Yakima, Best Western Plus Blazer, or look for suites with hot tub on Booking.com/Hotels.com filtered by amenity
- Budget range: $100-180/night
- **Book early if attending Balloon Festival** (Labor Day weekend)

## Saturday - Balloon Festival & Palouse Falls

### Early Morning: Yakima Festival of Balloons

- **Arrive by 5:30 AM** for the morning balloon launch (~6:00-7:00 AM)
- Watch dozens of hot air balloons inflate and lift off at dawn
- Coffee and breakfast from food vendors
- Head back to hotel to pack up and check out

### Mid-Morning: Drive to Palouse Falls (~2 hrs from Yakima)

- **Route:** US-12 East to US-395 South to SR-261 South
- Scenic drive through rolling wheat fields and shrub-steppe

### Late Morning: Palouse Falls State Park (1-2 hrs)

- **Highlight:** 200-foot waterfall plunging into a 110-foot-deep canyon carved by Ice Age floods
- Short paved walk from parking to the main overlook (~0.3 mi)
- Optional: hike down into the canyon for a closer view (steep, unmarked trail, use caution)
- One of Washington's most dramatic and least-visited waterfalls
- **Discover Pass required** ($10/day or annual pass)
- Midday light is good for photos

### Afternoon: Drive to Tri-Cities (~1.5 hrs from Palouse Falls)

- **Route:** US-395 South through the Columbia Basin
- Pass through the Hanford Reach area - vast open shrub-steppe landscape

### Late Afternoon: Tri-Cities Exploration

Choose from these stops:

**Option A - Nature/Hiking:**

| Trail | Distance | Difficulty | Notes |
|-------|----------|------------|-------|
| Badger Mountain (Sagebrush Trail) | 1.8 mi RT | Easy-Moderate | Panoramic views of Tri-Cities, stone marker shows Ice Age Lake Lewis high-water mark |
| Badger Mountain (Canyon Trail) | 3.25 mi RT | Moderate | Views of Columbia & Yakima Rivers, Mt. Rainier & Adams on clear days |
| Candy Mountain | 3.5 mi RT | Moderate | Wildflower meadows, rolling hills, Columbia Basin views |
| Twin Sisters Rock | 0.7 mi RT | Easy | Sacred basalt pillars, Columbia River views |
| Wallula Gap Overlook | 1.0 mi RT | Moderate | Dramatic cliffs, sweeping Columbia River views, raptor watching |

**Option B - History/Culture:**

- **REACH Museum** (Richland) - hands-on exhibits on Ice Age floods, Manhattan Project, and the region's rivers. $12 adults.
- **Sacajawea Historical State Park** - where Lewis & Clark camped at the confluence of the Snake and Columbia Rivers (1805). Easy walking trails.
- **Hanford B Reactor Tour** (advance booking required) - tour the world's first full-scale nuclear reactor from the Manhattan Project. Incredibly unique. Free but must reserve weeks/months ahead.

**Option C - Wine:**

- Tri-Cities has **250+ wineries** in the surrounding area
- **Red Mountain AVA** (20 min south of Richland) - premium wine region, multiple tasting rooms
- Popular: Kiona Vineyards, Quilceda Creek, Betz Family, Force of Nature
- Many tasting rooms in downtown Kennewick and Richland

### Evening: Return to Yakima for Balloon Glow (~2 hrs from Tri-Cities)

- **Drive back to Yakima** (US-395 N to US-12 W)
- **Arrive by 6:30 PM** for the evening balloon glow (~7:00-8:00 PM)
- Watch balloons inflate and glow in sequence - magical light show
- Enjoy the evening concert and festivities
- Head back to hotel

### Saturday Hotel (Yakima)

- Book a hotel with hot tub in Yakima (2+ stars)
- Same options as Friday (or extend Friday's booking for two nights)
- **Book early if attending Balloon Festival** (Labor Day weekend)

## Sunday - Half Day Exploring + Drive Home

### Morning (choose one):

- **Relax at hotel** - enjoy the hot tub and sleep in after early balloon events
- **Chamna Natural Preserve** (4.3 mi of trails, easy) - lush riverfront along the Yakima River, wildlife viewing, birdwatching
- **Amon Creek Natural Preserve** (2.0 mi, easy) - wetlands, diverse ecosystems, 25+ bird species
- **Richland Riverfront Trail** (9.3 mi, easy, paved) - bike or walk along the Columbia River
- **Clover Island** (Pasco) - lighthouse, river views, short walk
- **Farmers' Market** (Saturday mornings in summer) - fresh produce, food trucks, local crafts

### Head Home (~3.5 hrs)

- **Depart by noon** for a comfortable afternoon return
- **Route:** I-82 North to I-90 West through Snoqualmie Pass
- Optional stop: **Ellensburg** for lunch before the pass
- **Arrive Seattle:** ~3:30-4:00 PM

## Packing & Tips

- **Sunscreen & hat** - eastern WA gets intense sun, trails have minimal shade
- **Plenty of water** - arid climate, most trails are unshaded
- **Layers** - mornings can be cool (especially for balloon events at dawn), afternoons warm
- **Blankets or lawn chairs** - for the balloon glow and evening events
- **Discover Pass** - required for Palouse Falls and many state parks ($10/day or $30 annual)
- **Download offline maps** - cell service is spotty between Yakima and Palouse Falls
- **Book Hanford B Reactor tour early** - slots fill up months in advance if interested
- **Book Yakima hotels early** - Balloon Festival weekend is the busiest time of year

## Hidden Gems Worth Knowing

- **Saddle Mountain** (near Othello) - "Wahatis" or "saddle" shaped mountain, easy 1.5 mi hike to summit with 360-degree views
- **Umtanum Ridge** (near Ellensburg) - wildflower displays in May/June, rugged canyon hikes
- **Sun Lakes State Park / Dry Falls** (near Coulee City, ~2 hrs NW of Yakima) - 400-foot tall, 3.5-mile-wide dry waterfall carved by Ice Age floods. Only worth adding if you extend the trip.
- **McNary Dam fish ladder** (Richland) - watch salmon swim upstream (fall migration, Sept-Oct)
- **Coyote Canyon Mammoth Dig Site** (near Tri-Cities) - ongoing paleontological excavation (tours by appointment)
