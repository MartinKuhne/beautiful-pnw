# National Park Service (NPS) Sites in Washington

Washington hosts 3 Major National Parks alongside several historical sites, recreation areas, and trails managed by the NPS:

## Major National Parks

- **Mount Rainier National Park** — nps.gov/mora
- **Olympic National Park** — nps.gov/olym
- **North Cascades National Park (Complex)** — nps.gov/noca

## Historical Parks, Recreation Areas & Reserve Sites

- **Ebey's Landing National Historical Reserve** — nps.gov/ebla
- **Fort Vancouver National Historic Site (WA/OR border)** — nps.gov/fova
- **Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park (Seattle Unit)** — nps.gov/klse
- **Lake Roosevelt National Recreation Area** — nps.gov/laro
- **Lewis and Clark National Historical Park (WA/OR coastal sites)** — nps.gov/lewi
- **Manhattan Project National Historical Park (Hanford Unit)** — nps.gov/mapr
- **San Juan Island National Historical Park** — nps.gov/sajh
- **Whitman Mission National Historic Site** — nps.gov/whmi

## National Historic Trails & Scenic Routes Crossing WA

- **Ice Age Floods National Geologic Trail** — nps.gov/iafl
- **Lewis & Clark National Historic Trail** — nps.gov/lecl

# US Forest Service (USFS) National Forests in Washington

Washington falls under USFS Region 6 (Pacific Northwest). There are 6 primary National Forests headquartered or managed within WA, plus two major federally managed scenic areas:

## National Forests

- **Mount Baker-Snoqualmie National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/mbs
  (Covers the western slopes of the Cascades from the Canadian border down to Mt. Rainier)

- **Okanogan-Wenatchee National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/okawen
  (Covers the eastern slopes of the Cascades from north to central WA)

- **Olympic National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/olympic
  (Surrounds Olympic National Park across the peninsula)

- **Gifford Pinchot National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/giffordpinchot
  (South-central WA, includes Mt. Adams)

- **Colville National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/colville
  (Northeastern corner of Washington)

- **Umatilla National Forest** — fs.usda.gov/umatilla
  (Southeastern WA / Northeastern Oregon)

## Special USFS Monuments & Scenic Areas in WA

- **Mount St. Helens National Volcanic Monument** — (Managed under Gifford Pinchot NF)
- **Columbia River Gorge National Scenic Area (WA/OR border)** — fs.usda.gov/crgnsa
