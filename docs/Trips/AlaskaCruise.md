# Alaska Cruise Guide: Ketchikan, Skagway & Juneau

> **Comprehensive activity guide compiled from Reddit, Lonely Planet, Alaska.org, and travel experts. Includes individual booking options for independent travelers.**

---

## KETCHIKAN — Alaska's "First City" & "Salmon Capital"

Ketchikan is the most accessible port — many attractions are walkable from the docks, and it's the easiest port to explore without booking an excursion.

### 🏆 Top Activities

#### 1. Great Alaskan Lumberjack Show
- **What:** Thrilling live competition with axe throwing, log rolling, sawing, and more — consistently rated as the #1 must-do in Ketchikan
- **Duration:** ~1 hour
- **Price:** ~$50-70 per person
- **Booking:** Book directly at [lumberjackshow.com](https://www.lumberjackshow.com/) or via cruise line; seats fill quickly during peak season
- **Reddit consensus:** "Really fun, great for all ages. Females especially love it." — r/Cruise

#### 2. Creek Street & Totem Poles (Free/Low-Cost)
- **What:** Historic boardwalk built on stilts over the creek; explore authentic totem poles along the waterfront and downtown
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Price:** Free to explore; Totem Heritage Center ~$10
- **Booking:** Walk-up — no reservation needed
- **Nearby:** Southeast Alaska Discovery Center (~$5 for scavenger hunt with patch)

#### 3. Misty Fjords Flightseeing Tour
- **What:** Breathtaking 2-3 hour floatplane flight over the 2.3 million-acre Misty Fjords National Monument — snow-capped mountains, waterfalls, lakes
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Price:** $349-520 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - [Taquan Air](https://taquanair.com/tours/misty-fjords-flightseeing/) — locally owned, ~50 years experience, 6-passenger floatplanes
  - [Pirate Airworks](https://pirateairworks.com/) — excellent reviews for scenic flights
  - [Helicopter Air Alaska](https://www.helicopteralaska.com/) — helicopter options available
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Ketchikan/Misty-Fjords-Seaplane-Tour-from-Ketchikan/d942-6459MISTY) or [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/en-au/ketchikan-l1256/ketchikan-misty-fjords-national-monument-floatplane-tour-t625322/)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Bird's-eye view of pure wilderness — everyone gets a window seat with narration"

#### 4. Salmon Fishing Charter
- **What:** Troll for king salmon or halibut in calm Inside Passage waters — all gear provided, filets processed and frozen for shipping home
- **Duration:** 4-6 hours
- **Price:** $250-500 per person depending on trip length
- **Booking companies:**
  - [Ketchikan Fishing Company](https://www.fishingchartersketchikan.com/) — USCG certified, half/full/multi-day trips
  - [Ketchikan Fishing Charters](https://ketchikanfishingtrips.com/) — cruise-ship friendly pickup near docks
  - [Campbell Fishing Charters](https://campbellfishingcharters.com/) — private 4-6 hour salmon trips
  - Via [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/ketchikan/fishing) — local company, competitive pricing
- **Reddit consensus:** "Catch your own fresh salmon — the ultimate Alaska souvenir"

#### 5. Bear Viewing (Anan Creek or Neets Bay)
- **What:** Floatplane to remote bear-viewing sites in the Tongass National Forest — watch brown bears catch salmon at waterfalls
- **Duration:** Half-day (includes flight)
- **Price:** $450-700 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - [Taquan Air](https://taquanair.com/tours/anan-creek-bear-adventure-flightseeing/) — Anan Creek Bear Adventure
  - Via [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/interest/ketchikan/neets-bay) — Neets Bay tours
- **Note:** Limited daily capacity — book early

#### 6. Saxman Native Village & Totem Park
- **What:** 24 authentic totem poles at a living Tlingit village — one of the best totem collections in Alaska
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Price:** Free (donations welcome)
- **Booking:** Self-guided — 2.5 miles south of downtown on South Tongass Hwy
- **Lonely Planet:** "One of the finest totem pole sites in all of Alaska"

#### 7. Snorkeling with Sea Life
- **What:** 7mm wetsuits provided; surprisingly warm water reveals abundant sea otters, sea urchins, and fish
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Price:** $100-150 per person
- **Booking:** [Snorkel Alaska](https://snorkelalaska.com/) — highly recommended by Reddit users
- **Reddit consensus:** "Saw way more sea life than we were expecting"

#### 8. Salmon Bake / Crab Feast
- **What:** Authentic Alaskan seafood meal — fresh salmon, Dungeness crab, and sides at a rustic lodge or restaurant
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours (meal) + travel time
- **Price:** $60-120 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - Various lodge experiences — book via [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/)
  - Downtown Ketchikan restaurants (independent dining)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Combine with the Lumberjack Show for the perfect Ketchikan experience"

### 📝 Ketchikan Booking Tips
- **Easiest port for DIY exploration** — many attractions are within walking distance of the cruise docks
- **Book flightseeing early** — small planes fill up fast; 2-3 months ahead recommended
- **Save budget** — Creek Street, totem poles, and the Discovery Center are inexpensive/free
- **Fish processing/shipping** — all charter companies will clean, freeze, and ship your catch home

### 🥾 Walks & Short Hikes

Ketchikan's trails are embedded in the Tongass National Forest — dense temperate rainforest, old-growth Sitka spruce, and hemlock trees. Many trailheads are within walking distance or a short bus ride from downtown.

#### 1. Deer Mountain Trail ⭐
- **What:** Ascend through switchbacks to panoramic views of Ketchikan Lake, surrounding islands, and mountains — the most popular hike near the cruise port
- **Distance:** 3 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (steady uphill; manageable for fit beginners)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is 0.5 miles from downtown docks; take a short bus ride or walk
- **Reddit consensus:** "Cranks up the drama significantly — stunning views from the top"
- **Lonely Planet:** One of the top short hikes in Southeast Alaska

#### 2. Carlanna Lake Trail
- **What:** Short, easy trail leading to a serene alpine lake surrounded by old-growth forest — peaceful and family-friendly
- **Distance:** 1.5 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (gentle grade, well-marked)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead accessible via bus from downtown; check current access via [AllTrails](https://www.alltrails.com/us/alaska/ketchikan)
- **Reddit consensus:** "My favorite — short, easy, and beautiful alpine lake"

#### 3. Married Man's Trail / Salmon Walk
- **What:** Boardwalk and gravel trail following the creek upstream from Creek Street to a fish ladder observation platform — watch salmon "climb" the ladder during season (mid-July to September)
- **Distance:** 1 mile roundtrip
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, boardwalk sections)
- **Booking:** Free — starts near Creek Street (5-15 min walk from cruise docks downtown)
- **Pro tip:** Combine with Creek Street walk; bears sometimes spotted near the creek during salmon season

#### 4. Creek Street Waterfront Walk
- **What:** Walk the iconic historic boardwalk built on stilts over the creek — watch seals, explore shops, see salmon in the water
- **Distance:** 0.5 miles loop
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, boardwalk)
- **Booking:** Free — walk directly from cruise docks (5-15 min depending on berth)
- **Reddit consensus:** "The most famous street in Ketchikan — don't miss it"

#### 5. Refuge Cove Trail
- **What:** Coastal rainforest trail with ocean views, tide pools, and old-growth forest — less crowded than Deer Mountain
- **Distance:** 2-3 miles (varies by route)
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate
- **Booking:** Free — shuttle or short taxi ride from downtown to trailhead
- **Reddit consensus:** "Great alternative if Deer Mountain is crowded"

#### 6. Rainbird Trail
- **What:** One-way or out-and-back circuit through lush Tongass rainforest — starts behind the University of Alaska Southeast campus
- **Distance:** 1.5-2 miles
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is a short bus/taxi ride from downtown
- **Pro tip:** Combine with Carlanna Lake for a longer day hike

#### 7. Totem Bight State Historical Park Trail
- **What:** Follow the totem trail through the forest to the coast — authentic totem poles, tribal house, and beautiful coastal scenery
- **Distance:** 1-mile loop
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Booking:** Free — 10 miles from downtown; bus or shuttle available
- **Lonely Planet:** "One of the best totem pole sites in all of Alaska"

## SKAGWAY — Gateway to the Klondike Gold Rush

Skagway is a beautifully preserved Gold Rush town. The compact downtown is walkable, and the White Pass Railway is the iconic excursion.

### 🏆 Top Activities

#### 1. White Pass & Yukon Route Railway ⭐ (Must-Do)
- **What:** Historic narrow-gauge railway (built 1898) ascending 3,000 feet through dramatic mountain scenery — a Gold Rush icon
- **Highlights:** Dead Horse Gulch, Inspiration Point, Bridal Veil Falls, the 1901 cantilevered steel bridge
- **Duration:** 3.5-8 hours depending on route
- **Price:** $159-375 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - **Direct:** [wpyr.com](https://www.wpwr.com) — often cheaper than cruise line bookings; save $50-100 per person
  - Cruise line excursions available (more expensive but guaranteed return)
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Skagway/White-Pass-Scenic-Railway-Tour/d931-3011P14) or [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/skagway-l7053/activities/)
- **Pro tips:**
  - Reddit: "Book direct with the White Pass Railway to get a better rate — cruise lines mark it up significantly"
  - Sit on the **left side going up**, **right side coming down** for best landmark views
  - Consider the **Yukon Expedition** (goes into Canada) for the full experience
  - Combo options: train + dog mushing camp, train + salmon bake, train + bike tour

#### 2. Hiking the Chilkoot Trail
- **What:** Experience a portion of the legendary Gold Rush trail — moderate difficulty with spectacular views
- **Duration:** 3-6 hours
- **Price:** $159-300 per person
- **Booking:**
  - [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/skagway/) — raft float down the Taiya River included
  - Self-guided: Free access to portions; download trail info from [NPS Chilkoot Trail](https://www.nps.gov/chil/)
  - Ranger-led hikes available at the Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park
- **Reddit consensus:** "One of the most historically significant hikes in North America"

#### 3. Helicopter Glacier Tour & Dog Sledding
- **What:** Fly onto a glacier via helicopter, meet sled dog teams, and try mushing your own team across the ice
- **Duration:** 3-4 hours (including flight)
- **Price:** $500-900 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/skagway/) — multiple glacier options
  - [Alaska Tours](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/) — helicopter + glacier landing combinations
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Skagway) — compare operators and prices
- **Reddit consensus:** "Helicopter glacier tours in Skagway were absolutely epic — the highlight of our Alaska trip"

#### 4. Gold Panning
- **What:** Pan for real gold in the Taiya River — take home whatever you find
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Price:** $15-25 per person
- **Booking:** Multiple operators along Broadway; walk-up available
- **Reddit consensus:** "Fun, inexpensive, and you keep your gold — great for all ages"

#### 5. Skagway Museum & Self-Directed History Tour
- **What:** Explore multiple Gold Rush museums along Broadway — the Skagway Museum (on the 1st floor of the historic Customs House) is the finest in Southeast Alaska
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Price:** ~$10-15 per person for individual museums
- **Booking:** Walk-up — no reservation needed
- **Lonely Planet:** "Skagway Museum is one of the finest in a town filled with museums"

#### 6. Scenic Hiking (Free)
- **What:** Yukatania Point (2-mile roundtrip, moderate, sweeping views of Skagway and Lynn Canal), Tahkduana Trail
- **Duration:** 1-3 hours
- **Price:** Free
- **Booking:** Self-guided — trailheads are accessible from town; download maps at the NPS visitor center
- **Reddit consensus:** "Don't miss Yukatania Point — jaw-dropping views and free!"

#### 7. Dog Sledding/Musher Camp Visit
- **What:** Meet professional mushers and their husky teams without a helicopter — learn about the sport and history
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Price:** $60-120 per person
- **Booking:**
  - [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/) — dog musher's camp tours
  - Walk-up at local musher camps near the port

#### 8. Carcross / Yukon Day Trip
- **What:** Drive or take a tour south into the Yukon Territory — shadow the train route, see the Yukon Suspension Bridge, explore Carcross Desert (one of the world's smallest deserts)
- **Duration:** Full day (8+ hours)
- **Price:** $200-400 per person
- **Booking:**
  - [Skagway White Pass Tour](https://skagwaywhitepasstour.com/) — guided tour with Summit, Scenic Rail & Yukon crossing
  - Self-drive rental car available (check border crossing requirements)

### 📝 Skagway Booking Tips
- **Book White Pass Railway directly** — save $50-100 per person vs. cruise line bookings
- **Helicopter tours sell out weeks ahead** — book 2-3 months ahead for July/August sailings
- **The port is compact** — museums, gold panning, and downtown exploration are easily done on your own
- **Long port days are common** — Skagway often has the longest stays (7am-8pm), allowing for two activities


### 🥾 Walks & Short Hikes

Skagway is a **hiker's paradise** — extensive trail systems start just steps from the cruise ship piers. The NPS Klondike Gold Rush National Historical Park maintains detailed trail maps at the visitor center (free).

#### 1. Lower Dewey Lake Trail ⭐
- **What:** Popular trail through spruce/hemlock forest to a beautiful alpine reservoir and lake — amazing views of your cruise ship, downtown, and Lower Dewey Lake
- **Distance:** 0.5 miles one-way to lake (extend by walking around lake; total loop ~3 miles roundtrip)
- **Duration:** 1-1.5 hours (full loop)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (elevation gain, rocky terrain; doable for beginners at a leisurely pace)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is 446 ft from the Train Depot (easy walk from cruise docks; follow signs for Dewey Lake Trailhead)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Easy to walk from the boat, not too challenging, and amazingly quiet — best hike in Skagway on your cruise"
- **Pro tip:** Connect to Upper Dewey Lake or Icy Lake trails for a full-day adventure

#### 2. Upper Dewey Lake Trail
- **What:** Extend Lower Dewey Lake hike to reach a second lake, a waterfall, wildflower meadows, snow-capped peaks, and a rustic (rumored haunted) cabin at the top
- **Distance:** 2.5 miles one-way from Lower Dewey Lake (first 0.5 miles shared with Lower Dewey Lake trail)
- **Duration:** 3-4 hours total from trailhead
- **Difficulty:** Difficult (grueling elevation gain; experienced hikers only)
- **Booking:** Free — follow signs at Lower Dewey Lake (keep left)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Among my favorite hikes in Alaska, but a leg buster for sure"

#### 3. Icy Lake & Upper Reid Falls
- **What:** Two lakes (Lower Dewey + Icy Lake), a waterfall, and abundant Pacific Northwest fungi — hikers report "so many shrooms"
- **Distance:** 2 miles one-way to Upper Reid Falls
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours total from trailhead
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Booking:** Free — follow Icy Lake/Upper Reid Falls signs at Lower Dewey Lake
- **Pro tip:** Pack bug spray — bugs are prolific in summer; watch for mushrooms (do NOT eat any!)

#### 4. Gold Rush Cemetery & Lower Reid Falls
- **What:** Short walk past the historic Gold Rush Cemetery to a plunging waterfall through the forest
- **Distance:** 0.2 miles to falls (2 miles roundtrip walk from town to trailhead)
- **Duration:** 1-1.5 hours total (including cemetery exploration)
- **Difficulty:** Easy (appropriate for young children — keep them away from the raging water)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is 1.5 miles from the NPS visitor center; walk from downtown or take a shuttle
- **Lonely Planet:** Gold Rush Cemetery is a fascinating historic site

#### 5. Yakutania Point & Smuggler's Cove
- **What:** Short loop hike through forest to a scenic point overlooking Taiya Inlet and Lynn Canal; continue to Smuggler's Cove (beach picnic spot)
- **Distance:** 2-mile roundtrip loop
- **Duration:** 1-1.5 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead on First Avenue; walk west past airport terminal to footbridge, then follow signs
- **NPS:** "Great place to go for a picnic, rain or shine"

#### 6. Skagway Footbridge Walk
- **What:** Easy walk along a road through forest to the Skagway Footbridge — witness Alaska's massive tidal swings (up to 30+ feet difference!) and find the Skagway welcome sign
- **Distance:** 0.75-1 mile one-way from cruise ship
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes roundtrip
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, paved road walk)
- **Booking:** Free — walk from cruise docks; check tide tables beforehand to see the tidal difference
- **Reddit consensus:** "Perfect quick walk to start your day in Skagway"

## JUNEAU — Alaska's Spectacular Capital

Juneau is the only U.S. state capital you can't drive to (you must fly or cruise in). It's the busiest cruise port in Alaska with the widest range of activities.

### 🏆 Top Activities

#### 1. Mendenhall Glacier ⭐ (Must-Do)
- **What:** Visit the 13-mile-long glacier terminating at the far side of Mendenhall Lake — blue ice, icebergs, Nugget Falls
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours (including 20-minute drive from downtown)
- **Price:** $35-50 per person (including shuttle) or $5-10 self-drive
- **Booking companies:**
  - **Direct:** Book shuttle at [Mendenhall Glacier Visitor Center](https://www.alaska.org/detail/mendenhall-glacier-visitor-center) — run by the state
  - [Alaska Tales](https://alaskatales.com/) — excellent reviews on Viator, cheaper than cruise line
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Juneau) — multiple combo tours available
  - Self-drive: Rent a car from downtown Juneau (Avis, Hertz, Enterprise)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Worth every penny — about $35 per person for the excursion and absolutely incredible"
- **Note:** Bear sightings are possible along the Steep Creek Trail — Lonely Planet mentions bears in the area

#### 2. Whale Watching with Harv & Marv's ⭐ (Most Recommended)
- **What:** Watch humpback whales, orca (killer whales), Dall's porpoise, sea lions, and bald eagles from a small boat
- **Duration:** 4 hours (includes ~1 hour roundtrip transfer)
- **Price:** $180-230 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - **[Harv & Marv's Outback Alaska](https://www.harvandmarvs-juneau-whale-watching.com/)** — **the #1 recommended operator on Reddit and Cruise Critic**
    - 6-passenger private boats or 49-passenger public tours
    - Phone: (907) 209-7288
    - Location: 8603 Hayes Way, Auke Bay, Juneau, AK 99801
    - Reddit consensus: "Harv and Marv's is THE whale watching company in Juneau — smaller boats, more time on the water, better service"
    - "I've seen orca every time — incredible, go outside the cruise line for a smaller boat"
  - [Juneau Whale Watch](https://www.juneauwhalewatch.com/) — another quality operator
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Juneau/Alaska-Whale-Watching-Cruise-3-Hours-on-the-Water/d941-60598P2) or [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/juneau-alaska-l171160/from-juneau-whale-watching-cruise-with-snacks-t234852/)
- **Reddit consensus:** "If you only do one excursion in Alaska, make it whale watching in Juneau"

#### 3. Mount Roberts Tramway
- **What:** Scenic aerial tram ride to 1,800 feet on Mount Roberts — breathtaking views of the Gastineau Channel, Chilkat Mountains, and Tongass National Forest
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Price:** $59-79 per person (roundtrip tram)
- **Booking:**
  - **Direct:** [Mt Roberts Tramway](https://www.mtroberts.com/) — book online to skip lines
  - Walk-up at the tram station (5 minutes from cruise docks via free shuttle)
  - Hiking trail from the summit to the cruise port (moderate, ~3 miles)
  - Via [Alaska.org](https://www.alaska.org/detail/goldbelt-tram) (note: Goldbelt Tram = Mt Roberts Tramway)
- **Activities at the top:** Restaurant, gift shop, hiking trails, bear viewing (seasonal)

#### 4. Helicopter Glacier Dog Sledding (Juneau)
- **What:** Helicopter flight to the Taku Glacier — the ONLY place in the world where you can dogsled on a glacier accessible from a cruise port
- **Duration:** 3-4 hours (including flight)
- **Price:** $550-900 per person
- **Booking companies:**
  - [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/juneau/dogsledding-on-the-mendenhall-glacier-via-helicopter) — Mendenhall Glacier via helicopter
  - [Alaska Tours](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/juneau/juneau-dog-sled-world-taku-glacier-helicopter-adventure) — Taku Glacier Dog Sled World
  - Via [Viator](https://www.viator.com/tours/Juneau) — compare operators
- **Reddit consensus:** "The helicopter to glacier dog sledding was the most unique experience of our Alaska trip"

#### 5. Glacier Gardens (Douglas Island)
- **What:** Take a cart ride up Douglas Island to a glacier viewpoint with panoramic views of the Mendenhall Glacier and surrounding peaks
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Price:** ~$30-40 per person
- **Booking:** Walk-up at [Glacier Gardens](https://www.glaciergardens.com/) on Douglas Island
- **Lonely Planet:** Part of the "best places to visit in Alaska" list

#### 6. Alaska State Museum
- **What:** Explore Alaska's history, Native culture, and natural history — one of the most comprehensive museums in the state
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Price:** Free (donations appreciated)
- **Booking:** Walk-up — downtown Juneau

#### 7. Salmon Hatchery & Salmon Bake
- **What:** Visit the Peratrovich Salmon Hatchery (free) or enjoy a salmon bake dinner (paid)
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Price:** Hatchery: Free | Salmon Bake: $40-80 per person
- **Booking:** Hatchery — walk-up; Salmon bake — book via restaurants or tours

#### 8. Kayaking in Auke Bay
- **What:** Paddle through calm waters with glacier and mountain views — excellent wildlife viewing
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Price:** $100-150 per person
- **Booking:** Kayak rental companies at Auke Bay — search for local operators; book via [Alaska Shore Excursions](https://alaskashoreexcursions.com/)

#### 9. Treadwell Mine Historic Trail
- **What:** Explore the ruins of a historic gold mine — a unique, free outdoor attraction
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Price:** Free
- **Booking:** Self-guided — Douglas Island, walk-up
- **Lonely Planet:** "Treadwell Mine Historical Trail" is listed as a Lonely Planet point of interest

#### 10. Whale Sculpture & Waterfront Walk
- **What:** See the iconic whale sculpture at the end of the cruise docks; walk the beautiful Juneau waterfront
- **Duration:** 30-60 minutes
- **Price:** Free
- **Booking:** Walk-up

### 📝 Juneau Booking Tips
- **Book whale watching early** — Harv & Marv's books out fast; 2-3 months ahead for peak season
- **Mendenhall Glacier is the #1 free/low-cost attraction** — combine with whale watching or glacier dog sledding for a full day
- **Rent a car for flexibility** — Juneau is larger than other ports; a car gives you independence to explore Douglas Island and beyond
- **Tramway lines get long** — go early in the morning to avoid crowds
- **Many combo tours available** — whale watching + Mendenhall Glacier, glacier + dog sledding

## 📅 Booking Recommendations

### When to Book
- **Helicopter/Glacier tours:** 3-4 months ahead (sell out fastest)
- **Whale watching:** 2-3 months ahead (Harv & Marv's especially)
- **White Pass Railway:** 1-2 months ahead (can sometimes book walk-up, but risk of sell-out)
- **Flightseeing (Ketchikan):** 1-2 months ahead
- **Fishing charters:** 1 month ahead
- **Budget activities (totems, museums, hiking):** Book on arrival — no reservations needed

### Direct Booking vs. Cruise Line
- **Save money by booking direct** — especially for the White Pass Railway (saves $50-100/person) and whale watching (Harv & Marv's direct vs. cruise markup)
- **Cruise line booking convenience** — no worries about missing the ship; cruise guarantees return
- **Hybrid approach:** Book high-value/high-risk excursions (helicopter, whale watching) independently via trusted operators; use cruise line for activities you want guaranteed return for

### Trusted Third-Party Booking Sites
- [Viator](https://www.viator.com/) — wide selection, reviews, price comparison
- [GetYourGuide](https://www.getyourguide.com/) — competitive pricing, instant confirmation
- [Alaska.org Shore Excursions](https://www.alaska.org/things-to-do/shore-excursions) — official state tourism site

### Packing Tips
- **Waterproof jacket & pants** — essential for all ports
- **Layers** — temperatures vary; mornings are cool, afternoons can be warm

### 🥾 Walks & Short Hikes

#### 1. Nugget Falls Trail
- **What:** Flat, well-maintained gravel path from the Mendenhall Glacier Visitor Center to a beautiful 100-foot waterfall
- **Distance:** 2.0 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (paved/level)
- **Booking:** Free — walk-up from Visitor Center (take city bus #5 from downtown to Glacier Spur Rd, or walk 1.5 miles from transit center)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Best easy walk in Juneau — combines glacier viewing with a waterfall"

#### 2. West Glacier Trail
- **What:** Hike to the face of Mendenhall Glacier through old-growth forest — much closer view than the Visitor Center photo point
- **Distance:** 2.5 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 1-1.5 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate (some mild elevation, rocky sections)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is at Mendenhall Glacier Recreation Area (same shuttle/bus as Nugget Falls)
- **Pro tip:** Combine with Nugget Falls for a 4-mile loop; allow 2-3 hours total

#### 3. Mt. Roberts Trail (Hiking from Tramway)
- **What:** Hike from the Goldbelt Tram Mountain House down to the cruise port through rainforest — spectacular valley views without paying for the tram hike alternative
- **Distance:** ~3 miles one-way (tram to port)
- **Duration:** 1.5-2 hours hiking
- **Difficulty:** Moderate (steady downhill; uphill return is strenuous)
- **Booking:** Take tram to top ($59-79) then hike down; or start at Basin Road trailhead (downtown) for a 2-mile uphill to Mountain House (free)
- **Lonely Planet:** "Breaks out above tree line for views of Juneau and the Gastineau Channel"

#### 4. Perseverance Trail
- **What:** Historic mining trail through dense Tongass rainforest — old mining remnants, moss-covered trails, peaceful forest setting
- **Distance:** 1.5 miles one-way (connects to Mt. Roberts Trail at Basin Road)
- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, well-marked)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead on Perseverance Road off Main Street (10 min walk from cruise docks)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Hidden gem — easy, quiet, and right in downtown"

#### 5. Gold Creek Flume Trail
- **What:** Leisurely walk along the historic Gold Creek Flume (built 1892 to bring water to mining operations) through forest to Gold Creek Falls
- **Distance:** 1.6 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 45-60 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, gravel)
- **Booking:** Free — trailhead is 0.5 miles from cruise docks along Eastganite Road
- **Pro tip:** Great rainy-day walk; completely covered by canopy

#### 6. Treadwell Mine Historic Trail (Douglas Island)
- **What:** Explore ruins of a historic gold mine — abandoned tunnels, machinery, and forest trails on Douglas Island
- **Distance:** 1-2 miles of trails
- **Duration:** 1-2 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy to moderate (uneven terrain, some rock scrambling)
- **Booking:** Free — accessible by short ferry ride from downtown Juneau (walk up) or shuttle
- **Lonely Planet:** Listed as a Lonely Planet point of interest

#### 7. Auke Lake Trail
- **What:** Scenic lakeshore walk through alder and spruce forest to a secluded lake — popular with locals
- **Distance:** 1.3 miles roundtrip
- **Duration:** 30-45 minutes
- **Difficulty:** Easy (flat, gravel path)
- **Booking:** Free — 15-minute bus ride from downtown transit center (bus #2 or #4)
- **Reddit consensus:** "Perfect half-day nature walk — very peaceful"
- **Comfortable walking shoes** — port towns require lots of walking/hiking
- **Binoculars** — essential for whale watching and wildlife viewing
- **Camera with zoom** — glacier and wildlife photography
