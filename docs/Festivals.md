## Notes

- **Dates marked "estimated"** are projected based on historical patterns (e.g., "3rd weekend of July" or "first two weekends in October"). Always verify with the official festival website as dates can shift year to year.
- **Month-long festivals** (Skagit Valley Tulip Festival) have event dates spread throughout the month; bloom times depend on weather conditions.
- **Weather-dependent events**: Balloon festivals are weather-dependent and may be postponed or cancelled on the day of if conditions are unsafe.
- **Two-weekend festivals** (Coachella, EDC Las Vegas, Georgia Peach Festival) offer multiple opportunities to attend; ticket types and lineups may differ between weekends.
- **2027 Special Milestones**:
  - **Northwest Folklife** celebrates its 55th+ year as a Seattle institution.
