# Weekend Trip: Seattle to Palouse Falls

## Best Time to Visit

- **Late April through June** - Peak water flow from snowmelt makes the 198-foot falls most dramatic. Wildflowers bloom across the rolling hills.
- **Early-mid September** - Golden wheat harvest season offers stunning photography against the brown-gold hills. Cooler weather, fewer crowds.
- **Avoid** mid-July through August (hot, dry, low water flow) and deep winter (roads can be icy, facilities may close).

- **Distance:** ~170 miles
- **Drive time:** ~2.5 hours via I-90 East
- **Depart:** Late afternoon after work

### Route Highlights
- Pass through Snoqualmie Pass (elevation ~3,022 ft) - scenic mountain crossing

## Saturday - Palouse Falls & Sightseeing

### Morning Drive: Ellensburg to Palouse Falls
- **Distance:** ~155 miles via I-90 E and WA-26
- **Drive time:** ~2.5 hours
- **Depart:** 7:00-7:30 AM for a full day

### Stop 1: Palouse Falls State Park
- **Main attraction:** 198-foot waterfall plunging into the ancient canyon carved by Ice Age floods
- **Hikes & Walks:**
  - **Upper Falls Viewpoint Trail** - 0.3 mi (easy, paved) - Primary overlook of the falls
  - **Lower Falls Viewpoint Trail** - 0.5 mi (moderate, some stairs) - Gets you closer to the base of the falls
  - **Canyon Rim Trail** - ~1 mi (moderate) - Follows the canyon edge with views of the Snake River confluence
- **Time on site:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Parking:** $10/day or WA Discover Pass required

### Stop 2: Lyons Ferry Park & Snake River
- **Drive from Palouse Falls:** ~15 minutes
- Confluence of the Palouse and Snake Rivers
- Short walking paths, picnic areas
- Historic ferry crossing site
- **Time on site:** 45 minutes

### Stop 3: Washtucna & Rolling Hills Scenic Drive
- Drive the back roads through the iconic rolling Palouse hills
- The winding country roads between Washtucna, Colton, and Palouse are themselves a scenic attraction
- Pull over at pullouts for photography - the terraced wheat fields are unforgettable

### Stop 4: Colfax (Historic Downtown)
- **Drive from Palouse Falls area:** ~30 minutes
- Charming small town with well-preserved early 1900s architecture
- **Whitman Historical Museum** - Local history, free admission
- Walk Main Street, grab dinner at a local restaurant
- **Palouse Prairie Coffee** or a local cafe for a stop

### Evening
### Hotel Suggestions (Colfax / Pullman area, 2+ stars, hot tub)
- **Comfort Inn Colfax** - Indoor pool and hot tub, 2-star, basic but functional
- **La Quinta Inn & Suites Pullman** - Hot tub available, ~20 min south of Colfax
- **Best Western University Inn (Pullman)** - Indoor hot tub, near WSU campus
- Book ahead and verify hot tub availability

## Sunday - Half Day Explore + Drive Home

### Morning Exploration (3-4 hours)

**Option A: Steptoe Butte** *(recommended)*
- **Drive from Colfax:** ~25 minutes
- One of the last remaining volcanic buttes in the Palouse
- **Summit elevation:** 3,612 ft - 360-degree panoramic views of the entire Palouse region
- **Hike:** 0.3 mi to the summit (short but steep, ~200 ft elevation gain)
- A National Natural Landmark - the view from the top is arguably the best in eastern Washington
- **Time on site:** 1-1.5 hours

**Option B: Palouse Falls via the Lower Snake River Road**
- Revisit the falls from a different angle via the lower access road
- Different lighting in the morning sun

### Drive Home: Colfax/Pullman to Seattle
- **Distance:** ~280 miles
- **Drive time:** ~4 to 4.5 hours via I-90 West
- **Depart by:** 1:00-2:00 PM to arrive home by early evening
- Route: Colfax -> Pullman -> I-90 West through Ellensburg and Snoqualmie Pass -> Seattle

## Packing List
- Layers (mornings are cool, afternoons warm; eastern WA has big temperature swings)
- Sturdy shoes for canyon trails
- Camera (the landscape is spectacular)
- Sunscreen and hat (limited shade on trails)
- WA Discover Pass ($30 annual / $10 daily) for state parks
- Snacks and water (limited services in rural areas)
- Download offline maps - cell service is spotty in the Palouse

---

## Notes
- Rural eastern Washington has limited dining and gas options after dark; plan accordingly
- Palouse Falls is most impressive with high water flow (spring); in late summer the flow can be a trickle
- The rolling Palouse landscape is best appreciated from elevated viewpoints like Steptoe Butte
- Fill up gas whenever you see a station - distances between towns can be 30+ miles
